Main Street Cafe is where Clanton comes to eat like it's Sunday at Grandma's. We cook Southern classics from scratch every morning, the way they're supposed to taste: hearty meats, fresh vegetables, warm cornbread and something sweet to finish.

Our menu changes daily, so there's always something new on the line. Come hungry, stay as long as you like, and leave like family.
